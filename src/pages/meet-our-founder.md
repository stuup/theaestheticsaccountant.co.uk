I have been working closely within the Aesthetics industry for over 5 years now. Having discovered so many inspirational, and utterly ambitious, practitioners on this journey. The whole industry fills me with such passion for my work. Becoming Yorkshire’s, Humberside, and Manchester’s market leader for Accountancy in the industry has been a highlight. We are now expanding our business and have since grown our little niche accountancy practice into a national specialist service. This elite medical profession is one to be recognised and celebrated not only for its business contribution but also for the drive and determination of the people at its core. Their dedication to excellence and sense of morality is a value I align with personally, our clients are some of this country’s most celebrated Aesthetic professionals and we are committed to continuing and developing new relationships as we continue to support our current client base.
